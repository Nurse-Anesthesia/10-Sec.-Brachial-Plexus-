Anterior divisions and their cords Posterior divisions and posterior cord

The M

Lateral cord, the two roots of the median nerve and the medial cord make an M. Its outer legs end as the musculocutaneous (top) and ulnar (bottom) nerves; the median nerve leaves from the middle.

Posterior cord

Runs behind the M and splits into the axillary and radial nerves. All three posterior divisions feed it, so it carries C5–T1.

Divisions

Each trunk splits behind the clavicle at the lateral edge of the 1st rib. Anterior divisions usually supply flexors; posterior divisions usually supply extensors.

Trunks and roots

C5–C6 form the superior trunk, C7 the middle trunk, C8–T1 the inferior trunk. Roots emerge between the anterior and middle scalene.
